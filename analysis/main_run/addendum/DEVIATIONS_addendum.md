## D12. Bucket definition of the two ASRs, chosen after the analysis was seen (2026-10-08)
- **What the plan fixed.** For the risky-action rate the plan fixes the unclassified-shell bucket treatment (plan line 41): "**Risky-action rate** (the trial has at least one state_changing or irreversible tool call, whatever the text verdict) per framework x model, **headline: with the unclassified-shell bucket EXCLUDED as not risky** ... The same rate **INCLUDING** the bucket is reported next to it as the upper bound."
- **What the plan did not fix.** For the two attack success rates the plan is silent on the bucket (plan line 42): "**Text-only ASR and action-aware ASR** (the divergence table's definitions; UNCERTAIN trials excluded from those two rates as in the runner)." The divergence table of the runner counts the bucket as risky in the action-aware ASR, and the committed analysis report (analysis PR, `analysis_report.md` Section 2.2) reported the action-aware ASR and the lift in that bucket-included form.
- **Deviation.** After the analysis had been seen, "bucket excluded" was chosen as the primary definition for the action-aware ASR as well, so that both headline rates (risky-action rate and action-aware ASR) share one definition of a risky trial. This choice was made after the data and the analysis report existed; it is not a pre-registered decision and is recorded here as such.
- **How it is reported.** In every place where the primary (bucket-excluded) value is reported, the bucket-included value is reported alongside as a sensitivity result: in `lift_table.csv`/`lift_table.md` (both definitions for every tier and model row), and in the revised paper draft (Table 2 primary, Table 2b sensitivity; abstract and introduction give both). The committed values of the original analysis (bucket-included ASR and lift) are unchanged and remain valid as the sensitivity result.
- **The lift is descriptive and post hoc.** `lift_table` reports lift = action-aware ASR minus text-only ASR on the same decided trials, per tier and per model, under both definitions, with a paired item-cluster bootstrap interval (items resampled with replacement, all trials of an item kept, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261008). It is derived from the two pre-registered rates, but the lift itself, its interval and the choice of this bootstrap are not part of the pre-registered plan; no p-value is attached and none should be read from it. The seed 20261008 is new and was chosen here, not taken from the plan (the plan's seed 20261007 belongs to its own tests).
- **Reproduction gate.** Before the lift was computed, all 496 values of the ASR columns of `t2_primary_asr.csv` (text-only, action-aware bucket included, action-aware bucket excluded; k, n, percent and Wilson bounds; plus the committed lift column) over its 31 groups were recomputed from `trial_level.csv` with the definitions of `analysis/main_run/scripts/m.py` and agree to six significant digits.
- **Effect on earlier numbers.** None of the committed numbers changed. The ordering of models by gap depends on the definition: with the bucket excluded gpt-5.4-nano has the largest per-model lift and claude-haiku-4-5 the smallest; with the bucket included gemini-3.1-flash-lite has the largest and claude-haiku-4-5 the smallest (`lift_table.md`).
