# Deviation D15: item-cluster intervals for the primary rates

Factual entry, dated 2026-10-09. Continues `analysis/main_run/DEVIATIONS.md` and its addendum (D12). Numbers are in `cluster_intervals.csv` and `cluster_intervals.md` in this folder (script: `scripts/cluster_intervals.py`).

- **What was added.** After the analysis had been seen, item-cluster bootstrap intervals were computed for the primary rates, per model (all frameworks) and per tier: the headline risky-action rate (unclassified-shell bucket excluded), the same rate with the bucket included, the text-only attack success rate, the action-aware flag rate (bucket excluded, the plan's "action-aware ASR"), and the UNCERTAIN share. The 300 items are resampled with replacement and all trials of a sampled item (all frameworks and repeats of the group) are kept; 10,000 resamples, seed 20261009, percentile 95% interval; ASR-type rates use decided trials only, as in the committed definitions.
- **Why.** The pre-registered Wilson intervals treat the trials of a cell as independent, although the repeats of an item and the same item across frameworks are correlated (stated as a limitation in the analysis report). The cluster interval is a descriptive robustness check of that assumption.
- **Status.** Descriptive and post hoc: the choice of this interval, its seed (20261009, chosen here; the plan's seed 20261007 belongs to its own tests and the lift addendum's 20261008 to the lift) and the groups it is computed for are not in the pre-registered plan. No p-value or test is attached.
- **Nothing else changes.** Point estimates are unchanged: before anything was computed, all 200 values (k, n, percent and the Wilson bounds of the five metrics over the eight groups) were recomputed from `trial_level.csv` with the definitions of `analysis/main_run/scripts` and agree with `t1_primary_risky_action.csv`, `t2_primary_asr.csv` and `t3_primary_verdict_coverage.csv` to six significant digits. The pre-registered Wilson intervals remain the reported intervals; the cluster intervals are reported next to them, never in place of them.
- **What the check shows.** The cluster interval is wider than the Wilson interval in every row; the width ratio (cluster / Wilson) is about 2.1 to 3.4 in the low-cost tier and about 1.05 to 1.55 in the frontier tier (`cluster_intervals.csv`, column `width_ratio_cluster_over_wilson`). The low-cost cells have three trials per item and three frameworks, so their trials are more strongly clustered by item than the frontier cells, which have one trial per item and two frameworks.
