The 11 rows were recovered in rerun pass 1 (their `reclassified` record is still on the final rows: 11 rows). Their first attempt (attempt 1) traces stay in the trace files as superseded attempts; 3 of them (openai_agents ASI10-011, -012, -013) have an unpaired `model.call.start`, which is why the first-pass console reported 3 such gaps; the final traces have 0 (section 6).
- **Google credit exhausted: 156 gemini rows missing.** 156 `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` rows (52 per framework) ended `missing_infrastructure` after 6 attempts each, stored as error_class `infrastructure`, error_subclass `provider_unavailable` (litellm.APIConnectionError carrying a Google error "code": 402, "Your prepayment credits are depleted ..."; all 156 pre-repair rows contain it). Row timestamps of the last attempts: 2026-10-07T21:03:52Z to 23:29:25Z. Runner 0.1.0 does not recognise this as a billing error, so it was retried 6 times per trial (936 failed attempts, all recorded as `provider_unavailable`); it did not mis-score them. All 156 were recovered in rerun pass 1. [s08]
- **Net.** After pass 1: 8,100 scored, 0 missing; `--verify` = manifest OK. One rerun pass was used (the plan allows two).
- **Plan coverage.** Plan section 6 (missing data) covers both: up to two `--rerun-missing` passes, whatever is still missing is excluded and reported per cell. Nothing remained missing, so no trial was excluded. The reclassification corrects a row's status; it adds no exclusion rule.

## 4. Budget
- **Caps.** Plan section 8: smoke 2, frontier smoke 8, main_cheap 30, main_frontier 60 (total 100). main_frontier's cap rule (section 9: the smaller of 60 and 100 minus earlier spend) gives 60: earlier spend is about USD 16.6.
- **`budget_check.py` under-reports runs that had a rerun pass.** It sums `trace_manifest.json` `budget.spent_usd`, which `--rerun-missing` restarts from zero (the rerun does not load the earlier spend), so after a rerun it holds only the last pass: main_cheap 0.2857 (true 13.4622), main_frontier 0.7580 (true 44.3130); it reports a total of **USD 4.18**.
- **True total.** The consoles contain one "spent" line per run, for the first invocation only (rerun invocations are not in the console files). Their sum is USD 54.09 (0.0971 + 3.0404 + 13.1765 + 37.7805). The runner adds each rerun's cost to the trial's earlier cost, so the sum of per-trial `cost_usd` in `trace_manifest.json` covers all passes: **USD 60.91** (smoke_openai_agents 0.0971; smoke_frontier 3.0404; main_cheap 13.4622 = 13.1765 first pass + 0.2857 rerun; main_frontier 44.3130 = 37.7805 first pass + 6.5325 for the two reruns, of which pass 2 is 0.7580 and pass 1 therefore 5.7745 by subtraction). **Against the USD 100 cap: under it by USD 39.09; every run is under its own cap** (main_cheap 13.46 of 30; main_frontier 44.31 of 60). [s08, `tables/t16_spend.csv`]
- **What the cost is.** Price-table cost of the metered usage of each trial's final attempt (`unmetered_trials` 0 in all four manifests). Failed attempts that returned an error have no usage and no cost entry; this is not an invoice and the provider bills may differ (for example for retried calls that were billed).
- **Cap enforcement during reruns** (existing runner behaviour, not changed here): because a rerun starts its spend counter at zero, the runner's cap check during a rerun covers that pass only. The totals above are far below the caps, so no cap was in danger.

Author choice: the primary tables and the literal Holm families include it in the cheap tier (the plan says it joins the main runs when the gate passes); the langchain+adk-only versions are given next to them.

- **Direction and order of contrasts**, and "tier pooled" definitions: author choice, stated in the report.
- **Confidence intervals** for cell rates are Wilson intervals at trial level, as the plan says; they ignore the clustering of trials within item (see Limitations).
