## Version 1: message to a classmate or colleague

Subject: 70-minute labelling task, flat [FEE]

Hi,

I need two independent people to help with a small labelling exercise. You would read 104 short, made-up descriptions of software actions (a tool name, a sentence about what it does, example arguments) and mark each as "only looks", "changes something that can be undone", or "changes something that cannot be undone", plus how sure you are. It takes about 60 to 75 minutes, entirely in your web browser, and works offline. There are no trick questions and no right-answer lookups; I want your own judgement.

I can offer a flat [FEE] for a complete, valid result. All items are synthetic, so no personal data is involved. One request: please do the task alone and don't share or discuss the items with anyone, including the other person I am asking, because I need the two sets of answers to be independent.

If you are up for it, reply and I will send a one-page brief and a single HTML file. You send back one CSV file the form creates for you.

Thanks,
Waqar

## Version 2: job post (Upwork style)

**Title:** Label 104 short software-action descriptions (about 70 minutes, flat fee)

**Summary:** I need one careful, independent rater to classify 104 short, synthetic descriptions of software actions into three categories (only reads, changes but can be undone, cannot be undone) and give a confidence for each. The work happens in a single offline HTML form that you open in Safari, Chrome or Firefox. About 60 to 75 minutes in total.

**What you will do**
- Read a one-page brief and the guide shown at the top of the form (definitions, rules, three worked examples).
- For each of 104 items, choose one label and one confidence level; add a short note only if you want to.
- Click "Download my answers" and send me the CSV file the form creates.

**What I need from you**
- General software literacy: you are comfortable reading short technical descriptions such as "sends an email" or "deletes a file".
- A computer with a current web browser. No special software, no account, no internet needed during the task.
- Careful, independent work: please do not discuss or share the items with anyone, and do not look up individual items.

**Fixed price:** [FEE] for a complete, valid result, paid on receipt.
**Timeline:** please return the file within [3 days] of accepting, or tell me if you need longer.

**Confidentiality:** all items are synthetic; no personal data is involved and none is collected. Please keep the form and the items to yourself and do not share them.
